Paste your own list

Paste words or alphagrams, one per line or separated by spaces. Saving makes a deck called “My list” and adds every entry to your cardbox for the current quiz type. Anything not in NWL23 is skipped. To build a deck from conditions, use New deck or Search.

Speed round

Find as many words as you can before the clock runs out. Type each word and press Return; the next rack comes when you've found them all, or press Return on an empty box to skip. Doesn't change your schedule.

Valid or phony?

A word appears; decide whether it would survive a challenge. Half are real words from the deck you pick, half are phonies. It's practice only and doesn't change your schedule. Valid: V, Y or →. Phony: P, N or ←. On a touch screen, swipe the card right for valid and left for phony. Space or Return for the next word.

Keys and notes

While studying: Return checks typed answers or flips the card, as do Space and 0–3 in flashcard mode. On the back, 0 Again · 1 Hard · 2 Good · 3 Easy (Space also means Good). In Games › Valid or phony?, V/Y/→ or a swipe right for valid, P/N/← or a swipe left for phony. 5 shuffles the tiles, F toggles fullscreen and Esc leaves the study screen. Anywhere else, / jumps to Look up and Esc closes it.

Quiz types: Anagrams, Anagrams + hooks, Hooks, Build, Blanks (letters plus one blank), Extensions (longer words containing the word) and Definitions each keep their own cardbox. Valid or phony? is practice and doesn't change your schedule; it mixes words from the deck with made-up words, real words with endings they don't take, and Collins-only words. Probability ranks use two-blank probability. Hooks appear either side of each word. A red · before or after a word marks an inner hook: it's still a word without that first or last letter, so ·LOBELIAS· means OBELIAS and LOBELIA are words. A red + marks words and hooks new in NWL23. In Look up, ? is a blank.

Lexicon: NWL23.
